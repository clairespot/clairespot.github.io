Pedagogical Consultancy

A Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA), Chiara Portinari is a designer–educator whose work is grounded in inclusive, compassionate, and justice-focused pedagogies.

Approach

Chiara supports institutions and educators to rethink learning environments through co-creation, critical pedagogy, and experiential methods. A key focus is dismantling traditional power dynamics between students and staff, encouraging shared ownership of learning, and embedding intersectionality within curricula, assessment, and institutional practice.

What Chiara Offers

  • Learning design advisory: shaping inclusive, values-led programmes and units; aligning outcomes, activities, and assessment with equitable practice.
  • Facilitation & feedback: constructive panels, collaborative conversations, and 1-to-1 support centred on care, agency, and reflective practice.
  • Evaluation & impact: co-developing frameworks that centre lived experience (see Situated Evaluation).
  • Transformative experiences: collective feedback, student-led archives, public-facing learning spaces, and voluntary engagements linked to unit content.
  • Reflective facilitation: ongoing personal evaluation of workshops and classes to improve inclusion and engagement.

Professional Recognition

Chiara is a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA), recognised for a sustained commitment to inclusive and innovative higher education teaching.

Selected Work

  • Industry engagement (LCC – UAL): advised the Knowledge Exchange and Employability team to redesign company visits as participatory, inclusive experiences for all students.
  • Evaluation & Impact Project (LCC Changemakers): co-developed a flexible framework using methods like body mapping and journey mapping to assess social justice initiatives.
  • Transformative learning experiences (LCC): designed collective feedback formats, student-led community archives, and public-facing learning spaces that extend learning beyond the classroom.
