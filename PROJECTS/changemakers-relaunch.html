Relaunch Event
Who are the LCC Changemakers and how can they support LCC students and staff to succeed in their university experience?

At the Changemakers Relaunch event, Chiara co-presented and walked the audience through the key curriculum-level projects led by the Changemakers team of the London College of Communication (LCC).

The event helped raise the visibility of the Changemakers’ work, while also fostering new collaborative opportunities and connections across departments at the institution.
