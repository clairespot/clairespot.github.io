Context

During the second season of Making Connections—an arts festival based in the Old Waiting Room at Peckham Rye Station—Chiara collaborated with Karla Rosales Garcia and Abbey Lew to co-host two community workshops and a multimedia exhibition focused on place, memory, and future aspirations.

Activities

  • Community workshops: hands-on sessions inviting residents to reflect and create together.
  • Multimedia exhibition: film, photography, and sound pieces presenting community research.

Focus of the EDI Evaluation

In examining the festival’s practices, the team explored:

  • Physical and linguistic accessibility of the art space, communications, and activities.
  • Networking channels and outreach choices for connecting with local communities.
  • Alignment with local needs and vision to inform ongoing EDI improvements.
