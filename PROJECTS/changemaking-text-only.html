Guiding Question

Can a student-driven digital gallery challenge dominant narratives of regeneration and displacement in Elephant and Castle?

About the Project

Changemaking Led By Students builds strong, mutually beneficial relationships between students of London College of Communication (LCC) – UAL and local organisations that support marginalised communities. The project integrates real-world community challenges into students’ learning experiences, creating a space where education meets activism.

Approach and Leadership

Led from conception to delivery by Chiara Portinari, the project ensures alignment with both student experience and community engagement goals. The process was guided by values of co-creation, justice, and accessibility.

Main Outcome

The project’s central achievement is a digital archive that collects and showcases student work on local urban transformation and social justice. The archive includes multimedia content, reflections, and links to partner organisations, helping to extend the life and impact of student projects.

Testimonial

“Great to have as a resource and archive to help platform work, inspire future students and also good for avoiding potential replication of work.”

— Sophie Rebecca Wall, Project Officer at Latin Elephant
