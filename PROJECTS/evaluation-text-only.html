Guiding Question

How can evaluation methods move beyond traditional metrics to authentically include the LCC Changemakers’ voices and experiences in measuring their impact?

Situated Evaluation Framework

The Situated Evaluation Framework centers lived experience and identity in assessing impact. Instead of focusing solely on conventional outcomes, it highlights the personal and collective journeys of students and staff.

Development Process

Co-developed with Saranya Satheesh and Jiayi Wu, the framework was built through collaboration and testing with LCC Changemakers. It combines on-paper activities with emerging digital tools, including an experimental VR prototype.

The goal is to enable Changemakers to lead their own evaluations using their perspectives and lived experiences.

Future Plans

Future plans include refining the framework, continuing to test its effectiveness, and sharing insights more widely. The aim is to contribute to more equitable and meaningful evaluation practices across education and social justice projects.
