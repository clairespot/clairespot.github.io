Overview

The Wall Project offers second-year Communications and Media students at London College of Communication (LCC – UAL) the opportunity to exhibit self-directed research on social justice themes on university walls. It transforms circulation spaces into living, participatory environments that invite belonging and reflect the diverse truths of the community.

Approach

Moving beyond static, one-way communication, the project uses co-creation and dialogic design to cultivate more inclusive, conversational cultures. Curatorial practices are used intentionally and politically to highlight social justice issues, elevate marginalised voices, and spark collective reflection.

What Students Do

  • Develop independent research on a social justice theme.
  • Co-curate wall-based displays (posters, prints, zines, notes, calls-to-action).
  • Engage peers and staff through prompts and participatory elements.
