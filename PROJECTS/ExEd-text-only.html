About the Workshop

Embodying Care in Pedagogy was co-designed by Chiara Portinari and Saranya Satheesh for the UAL Education Conference (ExEd25). The session invited educators to explore teaching as an embodied, emotional, and identity-informed practice. Using body mapping, participants reflected on how care, knowledge, and power are carried within the body and expressed in the classroom.

Approach & Methods

  • Body mapping: drawing the body as a site of feeling, memory, knowledge, and power.
  • Prompted writing: short reflections connecting lived experience to teaching practice.
  • Group dialogue: sharing insights and witnessing others’ perspectives with care.

Grounded in anti-colonial and feminist pedagogies, the workshop encouraged participants to consider where care lives in their bodies, what knowledge they are supported to share (or discouraged from sharing), and what it might mean to teach from a place of healing—for themselves and their students.

What Participants Created

Participants produced varied body maps—some playful, some minimal, some densely symbolic—depicting where care, tension, and knowledge reside, and how these energies move through their practice. (Images are omitted here to keep this version concise for screen-reader users.)

Outcomes

  • Personal insight into the embodied dimensions of teaching.
  • Shared language and practices for more compassionate, just pedagogy.
  • Ideas for integrating reflective, creative exercises into curricula.
