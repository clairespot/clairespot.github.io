Postcards to the Community

A community workshop, created with Liana and Latin Elephant, inviting local residents to share memories and hopes for the future of Elephant and Castle through handmade postcards.

Guiding Question

What memories endure in Elephant and Castle, and what visions of justice and belonging shape its future?

About the Workshop

Postcards to the Community formed part of Latin Elephant’s 10th-anniversary celebration. The workshop provided a welcoming space for local residents to reflect on the area’s past and imagine possible futures. Participants were invited to create postcards—combining words and images—to express what matters to them.

The session was organised by Liana—a collective co-founded by Chiara—to support creative and community-led approaches to urban change. Chiara contributed to process design and facilitation.

Approach & Outcomes

  • Creative reflection: postcards offered a simple, intimate format for sharing experiences and hopes.
  • Relational space: a small-scale setting supported meaningful conversations between participants and facilitators.
  • Community voice: contributions together formed a collective snapshot of memory, place, and belonging.
