Marghera nel Mezzo

A participatory research and co-design project exploring the social, cultural, and urban identity of Marghera, a suburb of Venice. The work was co-created with residents, expats, workers, artists, and local organisations.

Guiding Question

How can participatory practices foster a sense of territorial identity and counteract the detrimental consequences of capitalism?

About the Project

Marghera nel Mezzo invited community members into a collective journey of listening, memory-gathering, and imagination. Through open and inclusive processes, the project highlighted everyday experiences and challenges while celebrating cultural and social identity.

Methods

  • Interviews: conversations with residents and workers about life in Marghera.
  • Exploratory walks: reflective group walks through the neighbourhood.
  • Collective mappings: visual and dialogic tools to chart shared stories and aspirations.

Outcomes

The project culminated in a community art gallery, featuring photographs, videos, audio pieces, illustrations, and texts created by residents. The gallery is designed not just as an exhibition, but as a living archive where new contributions can be added over time.

This gallery offers a collective, participatory expression of Marghera’s identity: a space for dialogue, reflection, and resistance to the forces of displacement and erasure.
