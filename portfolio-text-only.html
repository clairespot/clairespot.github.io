Designing Care within Systems of Power

Role: Project Leader  ·  Theme: Pedagogy  ·  2026

A presentation on the invisible work of collective leadership — examining the tension between assigned authority and intentional decentralisation, and how care can be embedded structurally into project leading, facilitation, and design within the Situated Evaluation Framework (SEF).

Process

  • Team mapping — Ran team-mapping sessions with the SEF Design Team (2024–25 and 2025–26) to surface strengths, experience, and areas for development.
  • Naming the tension — Examined the pull between assigned authority as Project Leader and an intentional effort to decentralise and support collective leadership.
  • Structural care — Identified how care was embedded into project leading, trauma-informed facilitation, and design choices that reduced participants' stress and confusion.

Outcome

Argued that impact isn't always visible — sometimes it's the infrastructure that lets collective work happen; directly informed the Collective Leadership Framework.

Situated Evaluation Framework

Role: Project Leader, Co-designer, Evaluator, Facilitator  ·  Theme: Evaluation  ·  2024–2026

A two-year project to design, test, and embed a scalable evaluation framework for student partnerships, focused on improving social justice in higher education — developed with student researchers, alumni, and programme staff across two cohorts.

Process

  • Theory of change — Mapped the Changemakers programme's intended impact using systems thinking and theory of change methodologies.
  • Prototype & test — Co-designed and iteratively tested the framework with the 2024/25 cohort, refining tools and templates based on what worked.
  • Toolkit production — Co-produced a publicly accessible toolkit with guidelines, templates, and tutorials for future use.
  • Embed & hand over — Adapted and co-ran the SEF with the 2025/26 cohort; co-authored analysis and published findings.

Outcome

Framework now in active use across two Changemaker cohorts. Blog co-authored with AdvanceHE; chapter co-authored for Springer Nature (forthcoming).

Embodying Care in Pedagogy

Role: Co-designer, Facilitator  ·  Theme: Pedagogy  ·  2025

A body-mapping workshop co-designed with Saranya Satheesh for the University of the Arts London (UAL) academic staff and international guests at the 2025 UAL Education Conference, exploring how care can be embedded into everyday pedagogical practice.

Process

  • Design — Developed workshop concept and structure together with Saranya Satheesh, grounding it in both our design for justice practices and inspired by the Situated Evaluation Framework for the LCC Changemakers.
  • Facilitation — Ran the session for UAL academic staff and international guests, using participatory methods to open dialogue around care in teaching.
  • Reflection & documentation — Captured participant responses and shared reflections as part of ongoing work in compassionate pedagogy.

Outcome

Delivered across two workshop sessions to 15 academic staff and international guests; contributed to UAL's ongoing dialogue around compassionate assessment.

EDI Evaluation for Making Connections

Role: Evaluator, Facilitator  ·  Theme: Community Action, Evaluation  ·  2024

A community EDI evaluation project in Peckham, London, exploring the past, present, and future of a local community initiative through participatory research and creative methods.

Process

  • Community interviews — Co-designed and ran interviews with local community members, listening for patterns and lived experience.
  • Workshops — Co-facilitated community workshops to deepen findings and invite collective reflection on the project's impact.
  • Street photography — Conducted a research photography session with the design team to document the neighbourhood's visual culture.
  • Public exhibition — Co-designed and ran an exhibition sharing research findings with the community and including a live workshop.

Outcome

Evaluated two seasons of Making Connections through 53 audience responses and two community archive workshops (17 participants total) at the Old Waiting Room, Peckham Rye Station; delivered three recommendations — more accessible community-centred events, youth engagement, and diversifying event locations — to the organising team.

marghera nel mezzo

role: project leader, curator, researcher  ·  theme: collective memory & place  ·  2023–ongoing

a grassroots social impact project in marghera, italy, researching and documenting the living culture of a neighbourhood through the eyes of its residents, expats, artists, and local organisations.

process

  • research walks — designed and led group walks through the neighbourhood as a method of collective observation and conversation.
  • artist interviews — conducted in-depth interviews with local artists to understand how they relate to the area and its history.
  • community collaboration — collaborated with residents, expats, community experts, and local organisations to build a plural picture of the place.
  • digital gallery — co-designed a publicly accessible grassroots digital gallery to share findings and give voice to the community.

outcome

128 residents surveyed and three community experts interviewed, alongside two public research walks; findings shaped a digital archive of marghera's stories, now live, with qr-coded totems currently being produced for distribution to local collaborators and shops.

global agreement on freedom of movement

role: co-researcher  ·  theme: freedom, care & possible futures  ·  2023

a speculative social impact project exploring what a global agreement on freedom of movement might look and feel like — through research, co-design, and a creative interactive performance.

process

  • primary research — co-designed and conducted interviews following design justice principles to understand people's experiences of movement.
  • co-design sessions — facilitated creative workshops and co-design sessions to develop the project's speculative proposals.
  • interactive performance — co-designed and participated in a creative interactive performance bringing the research to life.
  • data system — designed and crafted a data-gathering and data-displaying system to make the findings visible and engaging.

outcome

a costumed street performance staged in saint mary's churchyard, where 63 passersby cast written reflections on freedom of movement into a moon-shaped "polling station"; documented through a dedicated instagram page and a participant mailing list.
