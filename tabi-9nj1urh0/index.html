Where to tap

  • Suica works on every train, subway and bus on your route, Kintetsu included, and at konbini and vending machines.
  • Topping up is cash only: station ticket machines, fare adjustment machines or the till at 7-Eleven.
  • Backup: a contactless bank card or Google Pay opens the gates on Tokyo Metro, Toei, Osaka Metro and Kintetsu. It doesn't work on JR, or on Kyoto's buses and subway.
