About Chiara

“You brought care.” A teammate, on the SEF project

I'm a consultant, evaluator, and associate lecturer working at the intersection of education and social justice. I've worked as an LCC Changemaker since 2023, leading projects like the Wall Project and the Situated Evaluation Framework (SEF). In 2025 I became a Fellow of Advance HE and an Associate Lecturer on the MA Design for Social Innovation and Sustainable Futures. My writing on student partnership and inclusion has been published by Wonkhe and AdvanceHE.

about claire

“claire is not a tree. when it is needed, she can move around, she can question herself and change plans.” a classmate, from art school

i'm an experimenter, speculator, and wayfinder playing at the intersection of community and social justice. trained in visual and performing arts, my creative practice moves through crafts and storytelling — first alone, like for claire cares and the way things live, then with others, like for marghera nel mezzo and displaced | designers.

in 2024 and 2025, i also co-founded liana and vida, two social purpose creative collectives.
