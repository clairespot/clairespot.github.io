About

Chiara Portinari (she/they) is a social innovation designer, educator, and consultant based in London.

With a Master's in Design for Social Innovation and Sustainable Futures from the London College of Communication (LCC) – UAL, Chiara’s practice explores the intersections of creative resistance, speculative repair, and collective imagination as tools for transformative change.

Areas of Practice

  • 1. Innovative Pedagogies

    Both an educator and pedagogy evaluator, Chiara designs and facilitates participatory learning experiences that question dominant knowledge systems and cultivate liberatory, community-based approaches to education.

  • 2. Collective Curations

    Chiara co-designs collective curatorial processes, centering grassroots voices and artistic practices to activate spaces of shared authorship, reflection, and resistance.

  • 3. Communities & Events

    Through workshops, installations, talks, and facilitation, Chiara invites communities to feel, think, and build together, disrupting the status quo and envisioning alternative futures grounded in equity, care, and sustainability.
