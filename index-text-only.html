This portfolio moves between two related practices: Chiara Portinari's work in education, evaluation, and institutional consultancy, and claire's creative and community-based practice. Both are below, one after the other. Chiara Portinari and claire are the same person.

Chiara — Education & Institutional Practice

Helping institutions turn values into practice

I design, run, and evaluate participatory projects — focus groups, workshops, evaluation frameworks — that turn inclusion commitments into something real.

Portfolio highlights

  • Situated Evaluation Framework — An evaluation framework for student partnerships, built with two cohorts of researchers.
  • Embodying Care in Pedagogy — A body-mapping workshop exploring how care can live inside everyday teaching.
  • Changemaking Led by Students Archive — A prototype archive connecting student changemaking to real community needs.
  • Wall Project — A curriculum project giving students space to make their work publicly visible.
  • EDI Evaluation for Making Connections — A community EDI evaluation exploring the past, present, and future of a local initiative.
  • Postcards to the Community — Co-designing postcards with a local community to foster connection and shared authorship.

claire — creative & community practice

making things with people

creative and reflective arts for grassroots projects — illustration, film, zines, community workshops — rooted in care, place, and the people they're about.

portfolio highlights

  • marghera nel mezzo — documenting the living culture of a neighbourhood through the eyes of the people who know it.
  • bar cosmos — exhibition design for a graduate show, built through collaboration across departments.
  • the line's wellbeing walk — exploring wellbeing along a public art walk through london.
  • global agreement on freedom of movement — a speculative project imagining what freedom of movement could look and feel like.
  • claire cares — an illustrated collection documenting the everyday life and labour of a foreign nanny.
  • the way things live — exploring what our everyday objects mean to us, and how they age alongside us.
