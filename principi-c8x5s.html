Constitution Flow

Rap Articles 1 to 12 over a beat until they stick. Pick an article, start on Read, then hide more of the words each time you go round.

How to practise

  1. Learn one article at a time. Two rounds on Read at Slow speed, saying it out loud.
  2. Switch to Gaps. Say the missing word before it turns green.
  3. Move to Initials, then Solo, and speed up to Normal.
  4. Once a few articles are solid, use All 12 to run through them in order.

No sound on an iPhone? Turn off silent mode with the switch on the side of the phone.
